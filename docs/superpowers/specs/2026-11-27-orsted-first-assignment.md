# Orsted’s first assignment

An adult member of Orsted’s faction can open **A lead in the ledger** in Optional activities. Investigating the discrepancy opens a report to finish from the same activity. Leaving the lead for later keeps it available. Both decisions require the player even in Watch mode.

Submitting the full report earns up to 8 faction trust. Protecting the witness earns up to 4. Trust stops at 100 and the chronicle names the action and actual reward. Defecting during the investigation closes the mission without a reward. Completion and cancellation survive rejoining and reload. Existing members can start from their current saves. English and Thai use authored narrative and the existing reading-mode fallback. Recruitment, generic assignments, legacy lives and past lives retain their behavior.
