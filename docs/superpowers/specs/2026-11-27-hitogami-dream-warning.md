# The Dream’s Warning

An adult Hitogami apostle may open a once-per-life delivery warning in Optional activities. Hitogami asks for the delivery to be stopped without explaining why. Stopping it earns up to six faction trust. Letting it pass earns none. Only the player can decide, including in Watch mode.

A persisted dispatch recounts who benefited and who paid the price. The player can return through the same activity to read it. Defection before reading records the dispatch in the chronicle and closes the mission. Neither branch reserves a future decision slot or requires a random roll. English and Thai share the same rules and preserve the full localized account.
