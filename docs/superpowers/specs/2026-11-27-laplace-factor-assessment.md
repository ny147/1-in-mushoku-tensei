# The Laplace Factor Awakens

An active expanded character’s profile shows whether they carry the Laplace Factor. The specific manifestation and its drawbacks become visible after discovery. From age eight a mentor can offer a free assessment through Optional activities once training has produced an observed sign. The player may defer privately and return later. The assessment is manual and records the actual finding once. English and Thai support both the profile and the encounter. Existing adult discovery, aptitude assessment, birth odds, training and combat retain their rules.
