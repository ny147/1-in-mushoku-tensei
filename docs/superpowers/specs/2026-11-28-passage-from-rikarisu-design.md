# Passage from Rikarisu

The next origin story from [feature backlog #9](https://github.com/ny147/1-in-mushoku-tensei/issues/9)
gives a Migurd protagonist a way to earn the expensive fare from the Demon
Continent to Ranoa. The player chooses whether to keep flexible cash or commit
the value of the work to university travel. The outfitter and scene are
original game content.

## Approach

Use the existing Optional activities menu and University admission quote. A
cash-only bonus would let players save for any journey. A separate boarding
action would require handling tuition and relocation outside the university
flow. The outfitter's fare offer makes university travel the specific reward
while preserving the ordinary admission and funding process.

## The year's work

An active Migurd age 15 or older can visit the outfitter from a Demon Continent
settlement before graduating and while not enrolled at university. Existing
pending and queued decisions take precedence. Watch mode leaves both scenes
to the player.

Accept a free offer to work a year with the outfitter. Choose **Earn a living**
as the yearly focus. A year counts when it finishes in the settlement under
that focus while the player is alive and available for work. Custody, governing,
assigned service and active full-time training take precedence. Time away
and years spent on other focuses leave the offer open for a later year.
The regular economy continues during the term. The player may defer either
scene and resume later.

## Settlement and enrollment

After a qualifying year, visit the same Optional activity and choose one:

- Take up to **48 silver** as a one-time cash payment. The purse ceiling still
  applies.
- Ask the outfitter to cover the next university journey from a Demon Continent
  settlement. The reward covers the **120-silver travel charge** at current
  fares. The outfitter adds to the value of the cash wages to pay for the trip.
  Choosing the fare records a credit with the outfitter. Travel takes place
  through University admission.

University admission explains the fare credit in English and Thai and quotes
the reduced travel charge. The normal requirements and funding sources apply to
admission, tuition and living costs. Consume the credit only after successful
enrollment that uses it. A failed enrollment, a deferred offer, an ordinary
move, or admission already covered by a full scholarship leaves it unspent.
The credit can only be applied from a Demon Continent settlement. Cash
settlement, claiming the fare, and redemption each happen at most once.

## Continuity and verification

Persist the agreement year, credited work year, and settlement state in
expanded saves. Saves without an agreement remain eligible under the same
rules. Existing pending decisions remain valid. The story consumes no new
random draws. Verify settled and unsettled reloads, location and work-focus
gates, the two settlements, a rejected enrollment, and the exact quote and
loan after successful redemption. Exercise both languages through the
chronicle in an isolated desktop and phone-width browser.
